BUYING GUIDE

How to choose an EcoFlow power kit

A power kit bundles a power station with the extras most people end up buying anyway, such as a solar panel, an extra battery or a carrying bag, at a lower price than buying each item separately. Every kit shows how much you save.

Our kits are grouped by how people actually use them. Camping and hiking kits pair a RIVER or TRAIL station with a solar panel and a bag. Home office kits pair a RIVER 3 Plus with an extra battery for router and laptop backup. Home backup kits are built around DELTA stations, and larger kits around DELTA Pro stations suit long outages and off-grid properties.

To choose, start with the use case, then check the station's capacity and output against the devices you want to run. If you expect your needs to grow, pick a kit built on an expandable station so you can add batteries later. Every kit page lists exactly what is included.

Frequently asked questions

Are kits cheaper than buying the items separately?

Yes. Each kit is priced below the combined price of its parts, and the listing shows the saving.

What is included in a kit?

Each kit page lists the power station and every accessory in the bundle, such as solar panels, extra batteries or bags.

Which kit should I choose for home backup?

Look at the Home Backup kits built around DELTA stations for a general backup, or at the larger DELTA Pro kits for longer outages. Check the capacity and output against your essential appliances, or contact us for help sizing.
