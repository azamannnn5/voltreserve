BUYING GUIDE

How to choose extra batteries and accessories

This page covers extra batteries, cables, carrying cases, chargers, carts and other accessories for EcoFlow power stations. Each listing shows the models it fits, and compatibility matters: an extra battery made for one station will not necessarily work with another.

If you occasionally need more runtime but your station's output is enough, an extra battery is usually cheaper than buying a bigger station, and it keeps the base unit portable. If you regularly need to run more devices at the same time, you need a station with higher output, because extra batteries add capacity, not power.

Cases and bags protect your station in transit and in wet weather. Solar cables and adapters connect panels to your station, so check that the connector type matches both your panel and your power station before you order. If you are not sure what fits, contact us with your model and we will point you to the right part.

Frequently asked questions

Do extra batteries work with any power station?

No. Each extra battery is made for specific models, which are listed on its product page. For example, the RIVER 3 Plus can be expanded with a compatible extra battery, and DELTA Pro stations use their own extra batteries.

Should I buy an extra battery or a bigger station?

If you sometimes need more runtime and the output is enough, an extra battery is the cheaper option. If you need to run more devices at once, you need a station with higher output.

How do I check that an accessory fits my station?

Look at the Fits line on each listing or the compatible models on its product page. If you are unsure, contact us with your power station model before ordering.
