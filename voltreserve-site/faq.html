Frequently Asked Questions

General questions about ordering, our lineup, and what happens after you submit a request.

What's the difference between RIVER, DELTA, DELTA Pro, and TRAIL?

RIVER is our most portable line for camping and everyday carry. DELTA is the all-rounder — home office backup to weekend trips. DELTA Pro is built for whole-home backup, meant to replace a gas generator. TRAIL is ultralight and DC-only, for hikers who count every ounce. See the Buying Guide for a full breakdown.

Do I need an extra battery or a bigger station?

If you occasionally need more runtime but the base output is enough, an extra battery is cheaper and keeps things portable. If you regularly need to run more devices at once, you need a station with higher wattage output, not just more capacity.

How does ordering actually work?

Add products to your order, submit your contact details, and a chat opens with your order pre-filled — just hit send. We'll reach out to confirm the order and arrange payment — nothing is charged automatically.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash App, Zelle, Apple Pay, Chime, PayPal, and Revolut. Once your order is confirmed, your sales contact will share payment details and confirm which method works best for you.

How long does delivery take?

Smaller RIVER and TRAIL orders typically ship faster than DELTA Pro-tier items, which may involve freight shipping. Your sales contact will confirm exact timelines for your order. See Shipping & Returns.

What's the warranty on these stations?

All EcoFlow power stations carry the manufacturer's standard warranty. Warranty claims are handled in coordination with EcoFlow's support process — your sales contact can help facilitate this.

Can I compare two models before deciding?

Yes — use the "Compare" checkbox on any product card (on the Products page or any series page), select up to 3 models, then visit the Compare page for a side-by-side spec table.

Are these official EcoFlow products?

Yes. VoltReserve is an independent reseller of genuine EcoFlow products — not an official EcoFlow Inc. storefront, but all items are authentic.
