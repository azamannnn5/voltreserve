BUYING GUIDE

How to choose a TRAIL power station

TRAIL stations are built for people who count every ounce. They are ultralight, DC-only power banks with large capacity for their weight: the TRAIL 200 offers 192Wh at about 4 lb, the TRAIL Plus 300 offers 288Wh at about 5 lb, and the TRAIL 300 offers 288Wh at about 5.7 lb.

Because they are DC-only, TRAIL stations power phones, cameras, headlamps, laptops that charge over USB-C and other DC devices. They are not meant for wall-plug AC appliances. If you need AC outlets, look at the RIVER series instead.

TRAIL stations charge over USB-C, reaching roughly 80 percent in about an hour. The TRAIL 200 and TRAIL 300 do not include a dedicated AC charger, so plan how you will recharge before you leave. A small portable solar panel is a good match for multi-day trips.

Pick the TRAIL 200 for lightweight hiking and short trips, the TRAIL 300 for group camping or tailgating where weight matters less, and the TRAIL Plus 300 for tent camping where you want the extra capacity at a lower carry weight.

Frequently asked questions

Can a TRAIL power station run AC devices?

No. TRAIL stations are DC-only, so they suit USB and DC devices. If you need to plug in a standard wall-plug appliance, choose a RIVER or DELTA station.

How do I charge a TRAIL?

Over USB-C. Charging to about 80 percent takes roughly one hour. The TRAIL 200 and TRAIL 300 do not include a dedicated AC charger, so check what charger you will use.

Which TRAIL is best for hiking?

The TRAIL 200 is the lightest at about 4 lb and 192Wh, which covers phones, a headlamp and a camera for several days. For more capacity, the TRAIL Plus 300 gives 288Wh at about 5 lb.

Can I charge a TRAIL with solar?

Yes. Portable solar panels can charge TRAIL stations, which suits multi-day trips. See our solar panel page for panels that pair well with TRAIL.
